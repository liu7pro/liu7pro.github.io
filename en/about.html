About DomeOS Team

A team focused on slimming down and optimizing Windows

DomeOS Team is a team focused on slimming down and optimizing Windows. It is the predecessor of “DomeOS Technology” (founded by CangQiong). We try to reduce Windows resource usage without compromising features. DomeOS-Next and FireflyOS are already available.
