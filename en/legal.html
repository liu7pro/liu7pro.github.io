DomeOS Team Community Rules

Welcome to DomeOS Team!

0. Before we begin

This is a small group that builds third-party systems. If something is not perfect, please bear with us.

Tolerance, patience and listening are the rules of our group chat.

1. Misconduct and cybersecurity

We are not hostile to other third-party systems. We learn from each other and seek common ground while reserving differences. Do not post anything prohibited by the Cybersecurity Law of the People's Republic of China. Casual chat and off-topic discussion are allowed. Do not flood the group with explicit images. Additional rule: sexual jokes are prohibited; the first offense gets a mute warning, the second gets removal.

2. Feature and bug reports

Mention @wslaoliu7a in the group, or report in the DomeOS Team QQ group.

3. Compatibility issues

If the system freezes or black-screens while running, boot into WinPE immediately and reinstall the original Windows image. Issues caused by your hardware are not covered.

4. Installation and use

Only FireflyOS is currently available; DomeOS-Next will follow.

5. Download links

All downloads must come from the cloud-drive link in the group or LegspCpd's download site. “DomeOS Team” refers to a team that modifies Windows; for a complete, low-error system, use the official Microsoft image.

6. Chat content

No one has the right to interfere with the content of the chat, except for abusive or rule-breaking messages. Eliminate all forms of discrimination; if you disagree, communicate in friendly language.

7. No arguments or cyberbullying

Do not start arguments or cyberbullying. Comments such as “his work is like ***” that insult another person are not allowed. We remove people who do this. If you feel wronged, explain the situation to the group owner or an admin; we will help resolve it privately or in a separate group where appropriate. We do not take sides in abuse; we mediate and help restore a friendly atmosphere.

8. Feature suggestions for FireflyOS and DomeOS-Next

You can mention @wslaoliu7a in the group; DM @wslaoliu7a (if there is no response, please understand that the feature may be unnecessary or impossible to add); or email DomeOS-Team@outlook.com or FireflyOS-Next@outlook.com. Please use a friendly, humble tone rather than a questioning, doubtful or commanding tone. Bugs may be caused by Windows itself; cosmetic issues can be raised freely.
